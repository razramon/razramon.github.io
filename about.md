I'm Raz Ramon, a software engineer. By day I work on backend and LLM systems at Navina. Outside of work I build things around whatever interests me at the moment: deep learning, fast inference, GPU kernels, computer vision and edge devices. This blog is where I write up what I built and what I learned.

Before Navina I was a deep learning and computer vision engineer at the NBEL lab, working on object tracking with event cameras. Before that I was a software engineer at Elbit Systems, on network communication for embedded platforms, and an officer in the MAZPEN unit, on a real-time command and control system.

I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science from the Open University (magna cum laude) and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Tel Aviv University.
